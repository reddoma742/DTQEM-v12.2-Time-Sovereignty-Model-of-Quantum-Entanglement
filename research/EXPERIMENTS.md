# DTQEM v12.2 – Experiment Guide

## Experiment 1: Temperature‑Induced Decoherence
Set θ=180°, γφ₀=1000, γrel₀=300, vary T from 0 to 300 K → observe V drop.

## Experiment 2: Inverse Calibration
Enter target V, click Inv T or Inv γφ₀ → find hidden parameter.

## Experiment 3: Quantum Eraser
Increase t_obs (camera active) → V small; decrease t_obs (erase) → V returns.

## Experiment 4: Complementarity Check
Press Validate → ensures V²+D² ≤ 1 always.

## Experiment 5: H₂ Photoionisation Inspired (examples/h2_photoionization_inspired.py)
Bell state with/without decoherence, inspired by attosecond experiments.
